
# Prescription Refills

This document defines a synthetic prescription-refill support policy for capstone testing, not an actual Practo clinical policy.

Patients requesting a prescription refill should provide the relevant appointment details and an existing prescription when available. A refill request may require review and approval by a qualified clinician before a prescription is issued. Administrative support staff cannot authorize a medication, change a dosage, or guarantee that a refill will be approved. The patient may be asked to schedule a consultation if the clinician determines that a review is needed. Patients should contact an appropriate healthcare professional for medication-related concerns rather than relying on an administrative support agent.
