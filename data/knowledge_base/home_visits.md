
# Home-Visit Requests

This document defines synthetic home-visit booking guidance for capstone testing, not an actual Practo home-visit policy.

Patients may ask whether a clinician or eligible healthcare service offers home visits in their area. Availability depends on the service, clinician schedule, location, and any applicable eligibility requirements. The clinic must confirm the visit time, service scope, total fee, and any travel-related charges before the request is treated as confirmed. A home-visit request is not an emergency response service and must not delay urgent medical assistance. If home visits are unavailable or unsuitable, the patient may contact the clinic to discuss other appointment options.
