
# Emergency Visits

This document defines synthetic emergency-routing guidance for capstone testing, not an actual Practo emergency-service policy.

Appointment booking and administrative support channels are not intended to manage medical emergencies. Anyone experiencing a potentially life-threatening or rapidly worsening condition should contact local emergency services or go to the nearest appropriate emergency department immediately. Patients should not wait for a routine appointment confirmation or a chatbot response before seeking urgent help. The support agent must not attempt to diagnose the emergency, recommend treatment, or decide that delaying care is safe. If a conversation indicates an emergency, the response must prioritize immediate professional assistance over routine administrative information.
