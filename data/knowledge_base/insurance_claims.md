
# Insurance Claims

This document defines a synthetic insurance-support policy for capstone testing, not an actual Practo insurance policy.

Patients should confirm their policy coverage, network eligibility, and documentation requirements directly with their insurer before a consultation. A claim submission may require an itemized bill, consultation receipt, claim form, and other documents specified by the insurer. The support team can explain the administrative documents available from the clinic but cannot guarantee reimbursement or claim approval. Patients should retain copies of submitted documents and any claim reference provided by their insurer. Questions about claim rejection, exclusions, limits, or payment timelines must be directed to the insurer.
