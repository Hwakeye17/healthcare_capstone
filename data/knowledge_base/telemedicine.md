
# Telemedicine Consultations

This document defines a synthetic telemedicine-support policy for capstone testing, not an actual Practo telemedicine policy.

Patients may request a remote consultation when the service is offered by the selected clinician. The booking confirmation should specify the consultation time and the supported method for joining the session. Patients should use a private setting and a reliable connection when sharing information during a consultation. A clinician may determine that a remote consultation is unsuitable and recommend an in-person assessment instead. Telemedicine is not a substitute for emergency care, and an administrative support agent must not diagnose a patient or determine clinical suitability.
