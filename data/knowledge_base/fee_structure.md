
# Consultation Fee Structure

This document defines synthetic consultation fees for capstone testing and does not describe actual Practo prices.

The sample consultation fee for General Medicine is ₹500 per consultation. The sample consultation fee for Cardiology is ₹1,000, Dermatology is ₹700, Pediatrics is ₹600, and Orthopedics is ₹800 per consultation. These amounts are illustrative dataset values and may not reflect the fees of any real clinician or clinic. The applicable fee should be confirmed for the selected clinician and appointment before booking is finalized. Additional services, tests, or procedures are not included in these sample consultation fees unless explicitly stated.
