
# Second-Opinion Consultations

This document defines synthetic second-opinion booking guidance for capstone testing, not an actual Practo clinical policy.

Patients may request a consultation with another qualified clinician when they want an independent professional opinion. The patient should bring relevant medical records, previous reports, prescriptions, and other information requested by the consulting clinician. Availability, consultation fees, and appointment requirements depend on the selected clinician and clinic. The support agent may explain the booking process but must not compare clinical diagnoses or recommend which medical opinion is correct. Clinical interpretation and treatment decisions remain the responsibility of qualified healthcare professionals and the patient in consultation with them.
