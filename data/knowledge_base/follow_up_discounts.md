
# Follow-up Consultation Discounts

This document defines a synthetic follow-up discount policy for capstone testing, not an actual Practo promotion.

For this sample policy, an eligible follow-up consultation booked within seven calendar days of the original consultation may qualify for a 10% discount on the consultation fee, subject to confirmation by the participating clinic. Eligibility may depend on the original consultation, clinician, and service offered. The discount applies only to the eligible consultation fee and does not automatically cover tests, medicines, procedures, or other services. Patients should confirm the final fee and eligibility with the clinic before booking, and the support agent must not promise a discount without supporting evidence.
