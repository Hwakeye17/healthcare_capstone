
# Appointment Cancellation and Rescheduling

This document defines a synthetic cancellation and rescheduling policy for capstone testing, not an actual Practo policy.

Patients should use the designated booking channel and provide their appointment reference when requesting a cancellation or a new time. For this sample policy, patients are encouraged to submit cancellation or rescheduling requests at least 24 hours before the appointment. A requested change is not confirmed until the updated status or time is communicated by the booking system or clinic. If a slot cannot be changed, the patient may request another available option or contact clinic support. Any applicable cancellation charge must be disclosed by the clinic before it is applied.
