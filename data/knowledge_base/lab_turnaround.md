
# Laboratory Test Turnaround

This document defines synthetic laboratory turnaround guidance for capstone testing, not an actual Practo laboratory policy.

For this sample policy, routine laboratory results are estimated to be available within 24 to 48 hours after the laboratory receives the sample. Specialized tests may require approximately 3 to 5 business days, depending on the test and laboratory processing requirements. These are illustrative estimates rather than guaranteed deadlines, and the performing laboratory should confirm the expected reporting time. Delays may occur because of repeat testing, sample quality issues, holidays, or additional processing requirements. Patients should contact the laboratory or ordering clinic for an update about a specific test result.
