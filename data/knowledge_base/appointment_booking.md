
# Appointment Booking

This document defines a synthetic appointment-booking policy for capstone testing, not an actual Practo policy.

Patients can request an appointment by providing their preferred specialty, date, time window, and contact details through the designated booking channel. Appointment requests remain pending until the clinic or booking system confirms the slot. Availability depends on the selected specialty, clinician schedule, and clinic operating hours. Patients should retain their appointment reference and review the confirmed date and time before attending. If the requested slot is unavailable, patients may request an alternative slot.
